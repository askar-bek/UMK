1. Аннотация дисциплины / Дисциплинанын аннотациясы

This two-semester course examines British and American literature as the foundational dataset for modern Artificial Intelligence. Students will explore how the linguistic and cultural evolution of the English language, from *Beowulf* to the present day, informs the capabilities and limitations of Large Language Models (LLMs). Semester 1, "The Forging of a National Literature," focuses on the formative period from Old English heroic poetry to the works of Chaucer and the Medieval stage. Semester 2, "From the Renaissance to Modernism," investigates the language of Shakespeare, the King James Bible, the rise of prescriptivism, and the divergence of American English and World Englishes, analyzing how historical texts create patterns that AI systems replicate. The course aims to develop critical AI literacy, advanced analytical skills, and the ability to leverage a deep understanding of literary history in the age of AI.

2. Актуальность курса / Курстун актуалдуулугу

In an era dominated by AI, understanding the data on which these systems are trained is a critical strategic skill. This course is highly relevant because it positions classic literature not as a relic of the past, but as the source code for modern LLMs. By analyzing these foundational texts, students will learn to identify the origins of algorithmic bias, understand the principles of stylistic pattern recognition, and master the art of "prompt engineering" through a deep understanding of rhetoric and syntax. These skills are essential for future linguists, translators, and communication specialists who will be required to work with, critique, and improve AI-driven language technologies.

3. Цели и задачи дисциплины / Дисциплинанын максаты жана милдеттери

Goal: To provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the major periods, authors, and genres of British and American literature, using linguistic analysis as a primary tool for interpretation.

Objectives:
- To introduce students to the key literary movements from the Anglo-Saxon period to Modernism.
- To develop skills in literary analysis, focusing on how linguistic features (lexis, syntax, phonology) contribute to style and theme (ПК-8).
- To teach students to use scholarly resources and modern technologies to situate literary works within their historical and cultural contexts (ПК-7).
- To foster critical thinking and collaborative discussion skills through seminar-based analysis of primary texts (СЛК-1).

5. Перечень планируемых результатов обучения (компетенций) / Окутуунун пландаштырылган натыйжалары (компетенциялар)

In the process of studying the discipline, the student develops the following competencies:

  • СЛК-1: Ability to achieve goals in professional activities individually or in groups.
  • СЛК-2: Ability to evaluate and critique the outputs of AI models based on an understanding of their historical linguistic data.
  • ПК-6: Ability to perform linguistic and stylistic analysis of literary texts to identify patterns relevant to both human and machine understanding.
  • ПК-7: Ability to use reference materials and IT tools, including generative AI, for linguistic research.
  • ПК-8: Ability to analyze the connection between a literary work's language and its historical period.
  • ПК-9: Ability to synthesize literary analysis and knowledge of AI to construct a coherent interpretation of a text.

10. Контрольные вопросы для проведения промежуточной (итоговой) аттестации по дисциплине.
  1. Analyze the use of alliteration and kennings in *Beowulf*. How would these features challenge a modern AI translation model?
  2. Discuss how Chaucer uses linguistic variation for characterization. How could this data be used to train an AI to recognize different social registers?
  3. Explain the Great Vowel Shift. How does this historical phonological change affect an AI's ability to process rhymes in Shakespearean poetry?
  4. What were the main goals of the 18th-century prescriptive grammarians? How might their rules manifest as biases in modern grammar-checking software?
  5. Compare the representation of nature in British Romantic poetry and American Transcendentalist prose. What distinct lexical sets would an AI need to learn for each?
