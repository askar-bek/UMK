Цель и задачи дисциплины Цель - To provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the major periods, authors, and genres of British and American literature, using linguistic analysis as a primary tool for interpretation.
Задачи дисциплины:
- To introduce students to the key literary movements from the Anglo-Saxon period to Modernism.
- To develop skills in literary analysis, focusing on how linguistic features (lexis, syntax, phonology) contribute to style and theme.
- To teach students to use scholarly resources and modern technologies to situate literary works within their historical and cultural contexts.
- To foster critical thinking and collaborative discussion skills through seminar-based analysis of primary texts.
Описание курса This two-semester course examines British and American literature as the foundational dataset for modern Artificial Intelligence. Students will explore how the linguistic and cultural evolution of the English language, from *Beowulf* to the present day, informs the capabilities and limitations of Large Language Models (LLMs). Semester 1, "The Forging of a National Literature," focuses on the formative period from Old English heroic poetry to the works of Chaucer and the Medieval stage. Semester 2, "From the Renaissance to Modernism," investigates the language of Shakespeare, the King James Bible, the rise of prescriptivism, and the divergence of American English and World Englishes, analyzing how historical texts create patterns that AI systems replicate. The course aims to develop critical AI literacy, advanced analytical skills, and the ability to leverage a deep understanding of literary history in the age of AI.
Результаты обучения Upon completion of the course, the student will be able to:
СЛК-2: Evaluate and critique the outputs of AI models based on an understanding of their historical linguistic data.
ПК-6: Perform linguistic and stylistic analysis of literary texts to identify patterns relevant to both human and machine understanding.
ПК-7: Use reference materials and IT tools, including generative AI, for linguistic research.
ПК-8: Analyze the relationship between a literary work's language and its historical period.
