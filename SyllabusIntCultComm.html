Goals and Objectives

The goal is to develop students' intercultural communication competence.

Objectives include:

- Introducing students to the main theories and concepts of intercultural communication.
- Developing the ability to analyze the influence of culture on verbal and non-verbal communication.
- Teaching how to identify and overcome stereotypes and prejudices in intercultural interaction.
- Forming practical skills for adaptation and successful communication in various cultural contexts.

Abstract (Course Description)

This course is an introduction to the study of communication between people from different cultural backgrounds. We will examine how cultural values, worldviews, and norms influence communication processes, using cases from different contexts. Special attention will be given to the analysis of cultural differences, problems of intercultural misunderstanding, and strategies for building effective dialogue. The course combines theoretical knowledge with the analysis of practical case studies, interactive simulations, and analysis of modern media.

Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Define and explain key concepts of intercultural communication.
- Analyze intercultural interaction situations using theoretical models.
- Recognize and critically evaluate the influence of stereotypes and prejudices on communication.
- Compare verbal and non-verbal communication styles across diverse cultures.
- Apply knowledge to solve practical problems and conflicts in an intercultural environment.
