1. Аннотация дисциплины

This course is an introduction to the study of communication between people from different cultural backgrounds. We will examine how cultural values, worldviews, and norms influence communication processes, using cases from Asian, Middle Eastern, African, Russian, and Western contexts. Special attention will be given to the analysis of cultural differences, problems of intercultural misunderstanding, and strategies for building effective dialogue. The course combines theoretical knowledge with the analysis of practical case studies, interactive simulations, and analysis of modern media.

2. Цели и задачи дисциплины

Goal: To develop students’ intercultural competence.
Objectives:
- Introducing students to the main theories and concepts of intercultural communication.
- Developing the ability to analyze the influence of culture on verbal and non-verbal communication.
- Teaching how to identify and overcome stereotypes and prejudices in intercultural interaction.
- Forming practical skills for adaptation and successful communication in various cultural contexts.

3. Место дисциплины в структуре учебной программы

Prerequisites: A minimum TOEFL score of 60 or IELTS 5.0. These language proficiency requirements ensure that students have the necessary level of English to engage with academic texts, participate in discussions, and successfully complete written assignments.

Postrequisites: The successful completion of this course provides the foundation for more advanced subjects such as Sociolinguistics, Pragmatics, Theory and Practice of Translation, Discourse Analysis, and Linguistic Anthropology.

4. Перечень планируемых результатов обучения (компетенций)

В результате освоения дисциплины студент должен обладать следующими компетенциями:

  • ОПК-2: Способность работать в коллективе, толерантно воспринимая социальные, этнические, конфессиональные и культурные различия.
  • ПК-10: Способность осуществлять межкультурную коммуникацию в различных профессиональных сферах, демонстрируя знание культурных норм и ценностей партнёров.
